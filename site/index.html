❯ Why is XOM moving?

XOM is trading as a crude oil proxy, and it’s moved inversely to the broader market lately. Over the last 90 days it’s 0.73-correlated with WTI and 0.71 with Brent — tighter than the 365-day ~0.6, so the oil linkage has strengthened. Strikingly, its beta vs the S&P is −0.99 in this window: it rises when the market falls, behaving like an energy/inflation hedge (it’s also +0.66 with the 10Y yield).

The causal chain right now: geopolitics and supply news → crude → XOM. Headlines show US–Iran deal talks pressuring oil, offset by $5 diesel and rising pump prices. Exxon-specific catalysts: a 228% run has valuation debate going, new Africa capex, and earnings plus the Fed meeting this week.

Caveat: 90-day correlations, regimes flip — correlation ≠ causation.

Real session, unedited: Bloom picked the market-connector skill on its own, ran the correlation script over two windows (per the skill’s instructions), read the news flow, and answered with the chain. Every number came from the scripts.
